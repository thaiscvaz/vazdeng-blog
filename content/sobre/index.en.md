At **Itaú Unibanco**, I worked on data quality. Then I moved to **EBANX**, building ETL pipelines at scale. After that, I worked on a project for a US big tech company in Silicon Valley through **HCL Technologies**. Today I am at **Bradesco**.

My core stack is **Databricks**. Not because I read the docs. Because it's what runs in production where I've worked.

## Why this blog exists

In 2025 I joined the **Numerical Methods in Engineering** program at UFPR (Federal University of Paraná) as an auditing student, and in 2026 I was admitted as a full Master's student. My research is on AI-driven predictive monitoring using LLMs for operational systems.

Along the way I kept looking for writing on production data engineering in Portuguese and barely found any. Not the way I wanted to read it: with depth, from someone who actually shipped it, at a real bank, with real LGPD compliance constraints, SLA requirements, and regulatory oversight.

So I started writing.

## What you'll find here

The main track is **production data engineering**: Databricks, Delta Lake, Spark, dbt, Airflow. Real architecture decisions, mistakes I made and what I learned, with Brazilian context (LGPD, cloud cost in reais) where it matters.

The second is **applied AI for data**: agents and LLMs that survive production, with evaluation, cost and security at the center.

The third is the **Zero to Expert** series: fundamentals before the tool, one concept per episode, in order ([see the series](/en/series/zero-to-expert/)).

My master's research and side projects are not a separate track. They show up inside these three when they teach something about a production problem.

Posts are written in Portuguese first.
